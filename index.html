THINK LIKE A DESIGNER

A tiny HCI challenge 🎨

Human-Computer Interaction is about making technology easier and more pleasant for people to use.

🛍️

Which button is easier to understand?

Imagine you're shopping online. You want to add an item to your cart. Choose the button that clearly explains what will happen.

💡

Why does this matter?

Clear labels help users understand an action before clicking. This supports usability and helps reduce confusion.
